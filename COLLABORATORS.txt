from El Hizabri et al. (2026) was more complex than was appropriate for this 
assignment. I consulted with Prof Roy and Gemini AI on how to load and use 
wav2vec 2.0 and Pytorch to extract audio features using transformers and tensors.
I consulted with Prof Roy regarding pyramid pooling. And I used Gemini AI to help 
with literature review to determine which hidden state layers to extract. I 
generally found from reading research that wav2vec 2.0 layer stratification 
consists of spectral/acoustic features in lower layers, phonetic/prosodic 
features in middle layers, and linguistic/semantic featues in late layers. 
However due to limited time and computing power, I wasn't able to perform a more 
comprehensive comparison search across different layers and their respective 
combinations.
